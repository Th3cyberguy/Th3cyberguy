## 🎯 About Me

I currently work as a Solutions Senior Consultant at Deloitte, focusing on federal government projects and classified environments. My background includes extensive work in SOC operations, incident response, security automation, and mentoring the next generation of cybersecurity professionals.

**What drives me:**
- **Education & Mentorship**: Creating comprehensive reference materials, SOC playbooks, and cheat sheets to help junior analysts level up their skills
- **Tool Development**: Building practical DFIR tools like WinDEAS (Windows Detection Engineering & Automation Suite) to streamline investigations
- **Community Contribution**: Sharing knowledge through detailed documentation organized around frameworks like MITRE ATT&CK
- **Continuous Learning**: Always exploring new techniques in threat hunting, detection engineering, and adversary tradecraft

**Background:**
- Military veteran of 9 years with two combat tours in Afghanistan (12B - Combat Engineer)
- Experience leading DFIR solutions and security consulting across federal projects
- Expertise in AWS GovCloud, classified environments, and enterprise security operations
